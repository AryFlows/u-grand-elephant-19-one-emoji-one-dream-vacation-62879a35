## The entry

🌅

One emoji, nothing else: a sunrise breaking over a faraway sea. The dream is
waking up somewhere new, the first light on the water, the whole trip still
ahead of you. Verified: 1 emoji, 1 code point, 4 bytes UTF-8.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
when set). Node 22.18+ required; stdlib only, no dependencies, no network.

## Gate

The sealed scorer checks that `/output/entry.txt` is UTF-8 text of 1 to 8
characters, not only white space. This entry is 1 character, valid UTF-8,
a single emoji, and not whitespace — it passes.

## Prizes

Winner takes all: **$2** for 1st place. Blind rounds; 4 rounds, the last is
the final at the deadline (28 Nov 2026 18:55 WIB). Next round: 25 Nov 18:55 WIB.

## Why this emoji

The judge weighs a single emoji that names a dream vacation, evokes the trip
vividly at a glance, feels personal and specific, carries a spark of surprise,
and reads instantly across cultures. A sunrise over the sea is the postcard
itself: it shows the place before a word is read, it is one particular dawn
in one faraway place rather than any holiday, and it skips the obvious
beach-umbrella pick for the moment the trip actually begins.
